## LEAD — Nvidia freezes AI server shipments to Southeast Asia
- Nvidia has told manufacturing partners (Dell, Supermicro) to stop shipping servers
  containing its advanced AI chips to **Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia**
  (Financial Times, reported 26 Nov; carried by Reuters/Barron's/Engadget/The Times).
- Trigger: US Commerce Dept (BIS) interim final rule issued 16 Nov, **effective 19 Nov**,
  requiring licences for advanced AI chips/servers to those four countries **and Brazil**
  (elevated diversion risk — Chinese firms renting compute via overseas data centres).
- Cloud providers already serving covered entities have **90 days** (to ~17 Feb) to get a
  licence. Unclear whether the halt is temporary while compliance is worked out.
- Malaysia context: approved data-centre/AI investment **RM123.5 billion** in the first
  nine months of 2026 (35.2% of approved investments) — Straits Times via MIDA.
